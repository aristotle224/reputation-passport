Dispute Review

Dispute resolution is an open design decision in this protocol. Three approaches are under consideration:

  • Issuer self-correction — the issuing platform revokes and re-issues.
  • Arbitration multisig — a quorum of trusted parties can override.
  • DAO-style vote — token-weighted governance resolves contested attestations.

No approach has been selected yet. This placeholder exists so the console shell is complete. Implementation will follow once the design decision is resolved. See README.md → Open design decisions.
